# M140 to M141 Boundary

M140 freezes the accepted M131-M139 higher-autonomy surface for governed
red-team review only. It is contract-only, review-only, freeze-only,
deterministic, local-only, safe-ref-only, and no-effect.

M141 remains future Multi-User Product Boundary work. M140 does not add
multi-user runtime, account tenancy, identity federation, workspace sharing,
backend routes, Control Center controls, dependencies, beta release, alpha
release, production authority, or any authority-bearing execution path.
