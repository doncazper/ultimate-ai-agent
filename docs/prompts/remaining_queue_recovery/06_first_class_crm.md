# First-Class CRM Recovery Contract

Status: triage-ready recovery source. CRM M2 remains the implemented local
baseline; this contract grants no external CRM authority.

## Outcome

Advance UAA from CRM M2 into a relationship-first local CRM with explicit
Sales, Real Estate, Professional Network, Personal Network, and Private
Relationships workspaces.

## In Scope

- Accept the CRM domain, privacy, storage, migration, route, and render
  contracts after their ecosystem prerequisites are proven.
- Local encrypted persistence, workspace isolation, CLI parity, and governed
  local mutations in separately accepted child scopes.
- Typed projections into Today, Work Board, Calendar, Evidence, and Memory.

## Out Of Scope

- External CRM sync or writes, sends, calendar writes, account auth, silent
  merges, provider calls, browser automation, or production authority.

## Acceptance

- ECO-000 and required storage/threat-model gates are explicit merge blockers.
- Private data and governance evidence remain separated and redacted.
- Each implementation slice has migration, rollback, API, CLI, UI, and focused
  verifier evidence.
