## Outcome

Build UAA-native social planning, evidence, review, and dry-run publication
contracts that project through Work Board, CRM, Calendar, Communications,
Studio, Today, and Evidence.

## In Scope

- Platform-neutral campaign, asset, schedule, audience, approval, and dry-run
  receipt contracts.
- Review batches, redacted previews, and exact per-platform capability posture.
- Clean-room feature synthesis from public category patterns.

## Out Of Scope

- Live account auth, posting, messaging, browser automation, uploads,
  engagement actions, background scheduling, or production use.

## Acceptance

- Work Board and first-class CRM dependencies are explicit.
- Draft, approved, scheduled-plan, blocked, and published-proof states cannot
  be conflated.
- Each later publishing adapter requires its own authority, rollback, test
  account, redaction, and receipt gate.
