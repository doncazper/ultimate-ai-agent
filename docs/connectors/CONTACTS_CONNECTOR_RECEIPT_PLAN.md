# Contacts Connector Receipt Plan

M123 records a no-effect receipt plan for the Contacts Connector Contract
Refresh. The receipt plan may reference contacts scope refs, contacts boundary
refs, contact boundary refs, consent boundary refs, data classification refs,
retention boundary refs, actor-bound refs, baseline-bound refs,
source-calendar-connector-contract-refresh-bound refs, audit refs, replay
refs, and accepted checkpoint refs.

The no-effect receipt plan stores no raw contacts content, no contact card, no
address book data, no contact address data, no credential material, no account
data, and no network response. It is documentation and audit metadata only.

M123 adds no contacts connector runtime, no contacts account auth, no contacts
read, no contacts search, no contacts lookup, no contacts create, no contacts
update, no contacts delete, no contacts export, no contacts bulk export, no
raw contacts content, no credential handling, no network access, no backend
route, no Control Center control, no dependency, no M124 work, and no
production authority.

M124 remains future. M150 remains the planned v1.0.0-alpha target.
