# Email Connector Contract Refresh

Checkpoint M121 adds an Email Connector Contract Refresh. It is
contract-only, review-only, and safe refs only. The record is bound to the
M120 Production Authority Readiness Review and records email scope refs,
mailbox boundary refs, consent boundary refs, data classification refs,
retention boundary refs, actor-bound refs, baseline-bound refs,
source-production-authority-readiness-bound refs, user-bound refs,
workspace-bound refs, audit refs, replay refs, accepted checkpoint refs, and a
no-effect receipt plan.

The refresh is a connector planning contract, not an email connector runtime,
not email account auth, not email read, not email search, not email send, not
email write, not email delete, not attachment download, not raw email content,
not credential handling, not network access, and not account action.

M121 adds no email connector runtime, no email account auth, no email read, no
email search, no email send, no email write, no email delete, no raw email
content, no attachment download, no credential handling, no network access, no
account action, no model call, no memory write, no context injection, no
execution, no backend route, no Control Center control, no dependency, no M122
work, no broad autonomy, no beta release, and no production authority.

M122 remains future. M150 remains the planned v1.2.0-alpha target.
