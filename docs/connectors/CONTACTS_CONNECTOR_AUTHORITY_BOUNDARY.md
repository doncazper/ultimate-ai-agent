record uses safe refs and exact bindings to the M122 Calendar Connector
Contract Refresh. Contacts scope refs, contacts boundary refs, contact boundary
refs, consent boundary refs, data classification refs, retention boundary refs,
actor-bound refs, baseline-bound refs, source-calendar-connector-contract-refresh-bound
refs, audit refs, replay refs, and no-effect receipt plan refs are not runtime
authority.

contacts read, no contacts search, no contacts lookup, no contacts create, no
contacts update, no contacts delete, no contacts export, no contacts bulk
export, no raw contacts content, no credential handling, no network access, no
account action, no model call, no memory write, no context injection, no
execution, no backend route, no Control Center control, no dependency, no M124
work, no beta release, and no production authority.
