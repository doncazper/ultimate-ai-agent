# Contacts Connector Contract Refresh

Checkpoint M123 adds a Contacts Connector Contract Refresh. It is
contract-only, review-only, and safe refs only. The record is bound to the
M122 Calendar Connector Contract Refresh and records contacts scope refs,
contacts boundary refs, contact boundary refs, consent boundary refs, data
classification refs, retention boundary refs, actor-bound refs,
baseline-bound refs, source-calendar-connector-contract-refresh-bound refs,
user-bound refs, workspace-bound refs, audit refs, replay refs, accepted
checkpoint refs, and a no-effect receipt plan.

The refresh is a connector planning contract, not a contacts connector runtime,
not contacts account auth, not contacts read, not contacts search, not contacts
lookup, not contacts create, not contacts update, not contacts delete, not
contacts export, not contacts bulk export, not raw contacts content, not
credential handling, not network access, and not account action.

M123 adds no contacts connector runtime, no contacts account auth, no contacts
read, no contacts search, no contacts lookup, no contacts create, no contacts
update, no contacts delete, no contacts export, no raw contacts content, no
credential handling, no network access, no account action, no model call, no
memory write, no context injection, no execution, no backend route, no Control
Center control, no dependency, no M124 work, no broad autonomy, no beta
release, and no production authority.

M124 remains future. M150 remains the planned v1.2.0-alpha target.
