# Email Connector Receipt Plan

M121 records a no-effect receipt plan for the Email Connector Contract Refresh.
The plan may reference safe refs for email scope refs, mailbox boundary refs,
consent boundary refs, data classification refs, retention boundary refs,
actor-bound refs, baseline-bound refs, source-production-authority-readiness-bound
refs, audit refs, replay refs, and accepted checkpoint refs.

The receipt plan stores no raw email content, no mailbox content, no attachment
payload, no credential material, no network response, no account payload, no
model output, no memory write, no context injection, no execution output, no
backend route output, and no Control Center control state.

M121 adds no email connector runtime, no email account auth, no email read, no
email search, no email send, no email write, no email delete, no attachment
download, no credential handling, no network access, no backend route, no
Control Center control, no dependency, and no production authority.

M122 remains future. M150 remains the planned v1.2.0-alpha target.
