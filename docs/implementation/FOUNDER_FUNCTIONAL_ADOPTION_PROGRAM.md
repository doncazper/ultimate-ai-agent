# Founder Functional Adoption Program

Status: planned Queue V2 Wave 6 program. It begins only after Q31, the final
GoatCitadel comparison, is terminal. It grants no new runtime, provider,
browser, connector-write, shell, public-distribution, or production authority.

## Outcome

Move the existing UAA foundations from bounded implementation slices to a
private product the founder can use for sustained real work. The acceptance
standard is not that routes, schemas, previews, or representative components
exist. The normal product surface must complete the intended workflow with
real operator-owned data, durable backend state, understandable recovery, and
honest limits.

The program deliberately follows Q31 so its direct comparison of GoatCitadel's
chat and operator surfaces can inform the implementation sequence. UAA may
adopt demonstrated interaction patterns through original implementation that
fits its Python-core and authority contracts. It must not copy competitor code,
branding, unsafe defaults, or architecture that moves product truth into the
shell.

## Functional-Use Acceptance

A module is functionally adopted only when all applicable requirements are
proven on the exact accepted revision:

1. A founder can enter or import real private data and continue using it after
   restart without fixtures becoming primary truth.
2. The primary UI supports the normal lifecycle: create, inspect, edit,
   organize, search, archive, undo or correct, and recover.
3. Operator-critical work does not require raw JSON or a CLI workaround. CLI
   and API inspection remain available for parity and diagnosis.
4. Backend-owned contracts persist the product state; React-only state is
   limited to presentation concerns.
5. Import, export, backup, restore or rollback behavior is present wherever
   loss or corruption would otherwise strand meaningful work.
6. Failures, stale state, uncertainty, disabled capabilities, and the next safe
   action are readable in the normal surface.
7. Cross-module links survive restart and support a useful Today, Inbox,
   task, calendar, CRM, briefing, action, and evidence loop where applicable.
8. The workflow is exercised on the supported founder-private deployment and
   variable Mac/Windows client posture without claiming public distribution or
   production readiness.
9. A finite founder-dogfood scenario completes end to end with redacted
   evidence, including an important failure and recovery path.
10. Any remaining issue classified as polish is noncritical to completing,
    understanding, recovering, or trusting the workflow.

Visual refinement, alternate layouts, convenience shortcuts, animation,
microcopy preferences, and additional personalization may remain for later
use-informed polish. Missing lifecycle operations, misleading state, fixture
dependence, inaccessible recovery, data-loss risk, broken cross-module flow,
or a nonfunctional primary surface are foundational gaps, not polish.

## Q31 Input: Chat And Operator Experience

Q31 must directly observe equivalent chat-centered tasks in GoatCitadel and
UAA. Static screenshots may document visual presence, but do not prove
interaction quality. The comparison records:

- conversation layout, hierarchy, density, and readable use of space;
- composer behavior, attachments or context selection, keyboard flow, and
  send/cancel/retry affordances;
- streaming and progress visibility, tool/action disclosure, interruption,
  steering, and resumption;
- citations, artifacts, approvals, errors, uncertainty, and recovery in the
  conversation;
- navigation between chat, plans, tasks, evidence, memory, and settings;
- persistence across refresh, restart, and thread switching;
- accessibility, focus order, responsiveness, and steps/time to a useful
  outcome.

Each transferable pattern receives evidence, a transfer score, an `adapt`,
`study only`, or `do not borrow` disposition, and a measurable UAA exit test.
High-value accepted adaptations are owned by Q33 or another explicit Wave 6
item rather than disappearing into a comparison report.

## Wave 6 Sequence

### Q32 — CRM Functional Adoption

Use the first-class private CRM with real founder-owned records. Prove the
contact, organization, property, opportunity, activity, follow-up, priority,
search, archive/correction, import/export, backup/recovery, and private
multi-computer paths needed for daily work. External CRM writes remain blocked.

### Q33 — Founder Operating Loop Functional Adoption

Make Setup, chat, Today, Inbox, Tasks, Boards, Calendar, Knowledge, Memory,
Plans, Actions, and Evidence feel like one product. Implement the highest-value
Q31 chat and cockpit adaptations behind the same Python/API contracts and prove
normal work without operator-critical raw JSON or CLI fallback.

### Q34 — News And Source Intelligence Functional Adoption

Turn authorized sources into useful, provenance-bound and freshness-aware
signals. Prove intake, deduplication, clustering, ranking, preferences,
inspection, recovery, and delivery into Today and Morning Briefing. This does
not authorize unrestricted browsing, authenticated browser action, or treating
source content as instructions or authority.

### Q35 — Vertical Modules Functional Adoption

Exercise coherent founder-private workflows for Finance, Communications,
Social Intelligence, social publishing dry-run, Proposal Intelligence,
corrections, and improvement review. Q26 must be reconciled before this item can
complete. Bank or filing mutation, autonomous sends or publishing, and
unreviewed self-modification remain outside scope.

### Q36 — Cross-Module Adoption Closure

Run a finite set of representative daily-use scenarios across Q32 through Q35.
Repair foundational gaps discovered by those scenarios, verify cross-module
continuity plus backup and recovery, and create an evidence-ranked backlog for
the remaining noncritical behavior and presentation polish. Stop after the
bounded acceptance cycle; unusual edge cases without material founder impact
do not extend the item indefinitely.

## Program Boundaries

- Wave 6 does not grant a broad autonomy switch or new authority class.
- Existing policy, approval, route classification, OpenAPI, redaction, and
  Foundation Gate requirements remain mandatory.
- Founder-private dogfood is acceptance evidence for practical usefulness. It
  is not a public beta, production-readiness, security-certification, or
  supported-distribution claim.
- Every discovered gap is classified as foundational, noncritical polish,
  intentionally blocked, deferred with evidence, or out of scope.
- No item may become an endless parity or edge-case chase. The declared
  scenarios and measurable exit tests bound the repair cycle.
