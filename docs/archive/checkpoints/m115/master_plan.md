# Checkpoint M115 Master Plan

M115 Production Audit Retention Policy hardens production-readiness planning
without consuming a new product SemVer version.

## Scope

- Add production audit retention policy contracts.
- Bind the M115 record to the M114 account connector review record.
- Require safe refs, actor-bound refs, user-bound refs, workspace-bound refs,
  baseline-bound refs, retention policy refs, retention schedule refs, audit
  data class refs, redaction policy ref, deletion window ref, legal hold
  boundary ref, audit refs, replay refs, and no-effect receipt plan refs.
- Add docs, tests, static verification, documentation-integrity coverage, and
  Foundation Gate coverage.
