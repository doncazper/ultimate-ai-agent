review-only, deterministic, local-only, safe-ref-only, safety-modes-only,
disabled-by-default, route-free, and no-effect.

It records accepted M101-M144 refs plus enterprise safety mode, pro safety mode,
workspace boundary, role policy, authority ceiling, feature availability,
escalation policy, audit, replay, revocation, kill-switch, and no-effect receipt
refs.

It adds no Enterprise runtime, Pro runtime, plan enforcement, billing runtime,
billing plan boundary, account tenant runtime, role runtime, auth runtime,
backend route, Control Center control, dependency, beta release, or production
authority.
