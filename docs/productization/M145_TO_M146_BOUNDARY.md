# M145 to M146 Boundary

M145 ends at disabled-by-default Enterprise/Pro Safety Modes policy records. It
records safe safety-mode refs, workspace boundary refs, role policy refs,
authority ceiling refs, feature availability refs, escalation policy refs,
audit, replay, revocation, kill-switch, and no-effect receipt refs.

M146 remains future Billing/Plan Boundary work. M145 does not add billing,
plan enforcement, plan boundaries, account tenant runtime, Enterprise runtime,
Pro runtime, auth runtime, connector runtime, plugin marketplace runtime,
backend route, Control Center control, dependency, beta release, or production
authority.
