# Alpha Privacy Review Policy

M142 privacy review records are identifiers and summaries for review only.
Safe refs remain non-authoritative and do not grant access to private content,
accounts, workspaces, providers, or runtime systems.

Required bindings:

- accepted M101-M141 checkpoint refs
- privacy review refs, data boundary refs, disclosure review refs, consent
  review refs, and retention review refs
- audit, replay, revocation, kill-switch, and no-effect receipt refs
- explicit denials for privacy review execution, alpha privacy sign-off, alpha
  UI runtime, raw private content access, backend routes, Control Center
  controls, dependencies, beta release, and production authority

M143 remains future.
